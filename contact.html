Frequently asked questions

Who does KDH Biomedicals sell to?

We supply clinical diagnostic laboratories, CROs, clinicians performing point-of-care testing, pharmaceutical companies, and R&D and quality-control laboratories across India. We are a business-to-business distributor and do not sell directly to patients.

Can you help us introduce a new test in our lab?

Yes — that's a core part of what we do. Beyond supplying the product, we assist with technical matters and provide marketing support to help you launch a new test, overcome the initial teething troubles and popularise it among referring clinicians.

Do you provide technical support and training?

We do. Our team supports installation, validation, staff training and ongoing troubleshooting so your assays perform reliably from day one.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form above or email kdhbiomed@hotmail.com with the product or category you're interested in and your laboratory details. We'll respond with availability, specifications and pricing.

Are you open to new manufacturer partnerships?

Absolutely. If you manufacture advanced diagnostics and are looking for a route to market in India, get in touch — we'd be glad to discuss representing your products.
