Molecular diagnostics

Molecular Diagnostics

Sensitive molecular tools for detecting pathogens directly from blood and clinical specimens — particularly valuable in sepsis and difficult-to-culture infections.

Molzym MolYsis™ technology

Removes non-target human DNA and enriches microbial DNA from blood and other liquid specimens — dramatically improving the detection of pathogens by molecular methods.

MTB-DNA Blood

Based on MolYsis™ technology to enrich and detect Mycobacterium tuberculosis DNA from blood and other liquid specimens.

UMD-SelectNA™

A semi-automated solution for molecular analysis — detecting bacterial and fungal pathogens in clinical specimens for the diagnosis of bloodstream and other infections.
