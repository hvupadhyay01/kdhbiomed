Who we are

Laboratory medicine, made accessible

KDH Biomedicals Pvt. Ltd. is a Mumbai-based company engaged in the marketing and distribution of laboratory medicine products across India.

We serve clinical diagnostic laboratories, contract research organisations (CROs), clinicians performing point-of-care testing, pharmaceutical companies, and R&D and quality-control laboratories. Working as a connecting bridge for technologically advanced products between international manufacturers and Indian laboratories, our purpose is simple: to simplify, speed up, economise and add value to disease diagnosis — and, through that, to serve patients in need and distress.

We are a favoured vendor among leading reference laboratories because we don't stop at supply. We assist in technical matters and provide marketing support that enables labs to introduce new tests, overcome initial teething troubles, and popularise those tests for the overall benefit of the medical community and patients.
